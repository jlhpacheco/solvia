# Impact and differentiation: evidence and proposed pilot

Reviewed October 19, 2026. This document separates observed implementation,
published external facts and hypotheses that still need a campus pilot. It
does not claim measured savings, market-first status or campus authorization.

## The problem and proposed value

A student or visitor needs to identify the right permit, enter the right
vehicle, understand the total and know whether payment completed. A campus
needs records that connect the vehicle, permit and payment without treating
an interrupted browser page as payment truth.

Solvia demonstrates a focused alternative: describe a vehicle, review the AI
draft, confirm the catalog total, use PayPal Sandbox and retrieve a permit
after verified capture. The ten-campus CSV catalog is a dated research
fixture. It is not a live institutional pricing feed. All personas and permits
are demonstration records, not real parking authorization.

production system needs an accountable campus owner for prices and eligibility.

### What we can say about age

CSU Stanislaus's [2018 parking guidelines](https://www.csustan.edu/sites/default/files/2023-01/stanislaus-state-parking-guidelines-2018.pdf),
pp. 5–6, already name mycampuspermit.com for online purchases. The defensible
statement is **documented in use by 2018**. That is not a verified launch
date, and it does not show that the present interface, security or backend
has remained unchanged. The current homepage's 2018 copyright footer is
likewise not evidence of its last software update.

The useful distinction is a demonstrated workflow. Attractive styling,
online payment, visitor permits and PDFs alone do not establish a novel
category or a market first. No comparative speed, accessibility, security or
abandonment advantage has yet been measured.

## What makes the implementation substantive

Solvia's evidence goes beyond screens: real runtime Together extraction,
shared server pricing, actual PayPal Sandbox captures, retry/reference
checks, refusal/unapproved-payment guards and validated permit documents.
AI has no price or payment tool; the person edits the draft; the server uses
the saved catalog fee; a completed verified capture controls paid issuance.
See [Stage One evidence](STAGE-ONE-EVIDENCE.md), [testing](TESTING.md),
[payment results](SANDBOX-RESULTS-2026-10-19.md) and
[quality control](QUALITY-CONTROL.md) for versioned results and limits.

These boundaries make a non-trivial implementation. They are not proof that
the individual techniques are new, that all competing products lack them,
or that an actual campus has adopted Solvia.

## Proposed pilot: measure the benefit before claiming it

Recruit consenting adult students and visitors for a small usability pilot,
and a campus operations partner for the staff workflow. Use fictional
vehicles and Sandbox payments. Compare Solvia's AI and classic paths with
an agreed baseline; use the existing authorized campus workflow only when
the campus permits it. Keep tasks equivalent and alternate their order to
reduce learning effects. Report participant count, devices, failures and
uncertainty; a small sample is directional evidence, not a population result.

| Measure | Proposed method | What it would establish |
|---|---|---|
| Completion | Count participants who obtain the correct permit/document divided by all assigned participants. Define success before testing. | Whether the complete flow is understandable; include abandonment and failed payments. |
| Time and corrections | Time task start to usable permit; record median/range, initial wrong fields, manual corrections and remaining errors. | Whether AI actually reduces entry work without worsening saved vehicle accuracy. |
| Staff minutes | Observe support and reconciliation minutes for each task; distinguish one-off training from repeat work. | Whether clearer vehicle/payment records reduce operational effort. |
| Cost per issued permit | Divide attributable operating cost by correctly issued permits for the same period. Include hosting, inference, payment fees, licensing, support, integration, maintenance and implementation amortization. | A credible total-cost comparison; an inexpensive model request alone is insufficient. |
| Student clarity | Ask participants to identify campus, category, price, validity and whether payment completed. Record answers and short comments. | Whether the interface communicates the decisions it claims to clarify. |

Predefine the observation period and baseline, and publish the measured
results rather than selecting only favorable tasks. Do not infer retention,
enrollment, classroom funding, increased parking revenue or fewer citations
from completion-time improvements alone. A claim about instructional savings
also needs the district's actual funding transfers and accounting treatment.

## Concise judge-ready pitch

**Problem:** A student or visitor needs the correct vehicle permit, a clear
total and confidence that payment completed. Campuses need usable records
without avoidable corrections and support work.

**Innovation:** Solvia turns a vehicle description into a reviewable,
payment-verified permit journey. AI handles language, the catalog controls
price, the person confirms the details and PayPal supplies payment truth.
Its contribution is the focused combination and the visible trust boundaries.

**Potential impact:** The prototype demonstrates that flow with real Sandbox
captures and downloadable permits. We hypothesize that clearer review and
payment records can reduce corrections and support work. A campus pilot
must measure those effects and total operating cost before we claim savings.
