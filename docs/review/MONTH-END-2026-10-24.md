## Report and repair

The reported sentence was:

> i am coming over monday 10/26/2026 until the end of november 2026, every monday , Wednesday and Friday. ok

The previous parser recognized the start and weekdays but left the end blank.
It now fills **October 26–November 30, 2026**, with Monday, Wednesday and
Friday selected. Counting only those weekdays, including both endpoints,
produces **16 visits**. For the controlled Fresno catalog case, comparison
shows $16 in daily passes versus a $30 term permit. Those are campus-specific
catalog prices, not model-generated fees.

Month ends are calculated from the calendar, including leap-year rules.
A full numeric start date is required. A named month with an omitted year
uses that start's year; “end of the month” uses its month. The reader does
not use today's date or assume a year rollover. A stated endpoint year wins;
reversed, conflicting, malformed or longer-than-182-day ranges require review.
Abbreviations and a comma before the year are covered. Review caught and
fixed cases that could otherwise discard a punctuated or contradictory year.

Both the hosted reader and its labeled rules fallback use the same grounded
dates. Provider output must agree with the grounded weekdays and internal
week count; its schema cannot set dates, prices or payment actions. The form
continues to ask for dates and weekdays, without a weeks field. Reading,
comparison and choosing dates do not create a purchase.
