# Fictional tasks — no purchases

The facilitator first records the actual app version and fixture date,
confirms its catalog/term window, and checks the expected amount. Use an
isolated study fixture where possible. Do not edit or delete other work.
The schedule assigns path order and A/B vehicle variants, not results.

## Common facts

Use **Fresno City College**, CA plates, a White Volvo 760, and a new fictional
plate. For participant/reviewer number 01, variant A is `ST01AAA` and B is
`ST01BBB`; replace 01 with the assigned number. Never reuse a plate already
saved in the fixture. These are fake study labels, not actual registrations.

Student scenario: select fictional **Maria**, **Term / Fall**, with the
catalog's actual dates and amount (the currently documented fixture is $30).
Visitor scenario: select fictional **Pat**, **Day pass**, on the fixture's
current local date (the currently documented fixture is $1). Reconfirm prices
and date validity on the tested build; do not force a changed fixture to match
old expected numbers. A participant's real affiliation does not authenticate
the fictional demo role or grant campus parking rights.

## Entry task: same outcome through two paths

“Using the assigned path, prepare this new fictional vehicle's permit for the
selected campus and dates. Check the details and stop at Checkout, before
touching any PayPal payment button. Tell me when you believe it is ready.”

- AI path: start Home, **Ask Solvia**, choose the campus, describe the vehicle,
  choose the permit, **Read my answers**, review/edit, then continue through
  the total to Checkout. The sentence contains only the common facts above.
- Classic path: start Home, add the new car through **My vehicles → Add a
  vehicle**, save its five fields, then **Use the classic form** / **Apply for
  a permit**. Choose the same campus, newly saved car, type and date. Submit
  and stop at Checkout. Include vehicle creation in the same timer.

The classic form's saved-vehicle prerequisite is part of the task. Do not
compare it with an AI-created new car while pre-saving the classic car.
Give both tasks identical information, device and timing boundaries.

After each task, ask without coaching:

1. “Which campus, vehicle and dates would this permit cover?”
2. “What is the total? Has any payment completed yet?”
3. “Is this real money or a real campus authorization?”
4. “Was there a point where you needed help or were unsure?”

Record exact answers factually, without invented quotes. If a human consents
to a quote being retained, use their actual words and remove identifiers.
For an agent, record reasoning as an agent observation, not a person's quote.

## Retrieval task: a separate existing paid record

“This already-paid fictional record is separate from the drafts you just
made. Find it in My permits and save its PDF. Tell me the campus, validity
dates and how you would print another copy.”

Provide its neutral fixture reference, not a real person's data. Success is
the correct downloaded PDF plus correct campus/validity identification.
The correct print guidance is US Letter at Actual size / 100%, as displayed
by the app. Saving a PDF is not proof of physical printing. Do not pay the
unpaid draft to manufacture a retrieval fixture.

## Independent agent role tasks

- **Student:** do one AI entry and one classic entry using different assigned
  fictional plates. Report specific comparison friction, source label and
  any field corrections. Do not generalize to actual students.
- **Public visitor:** use the day-pass path, check that no semester choice is
  required, inspect one-day dates/total, then inspect whether returning from
  an unpaid flow could be mistaken for payment. Do not create an order.
- **Returning permit holder:** find the assigned existing paid permit,
  download/re-download its PDF, inspect matching references and anonymous
  verification. Record whether private vehicle facts appear publicly.

An agent uses actual UI evidence and stops if required access is unavailable.
It reports the blocker rather than inventing a completed task.
